OUR STORY

Charting a path to premium, alcohol-free enjoyment for those who value more in each moment.

Better Rhodes was started as an idea to bring the No- and Low-Alcohol beverages to market through the development of game-changing brands. Our marketplace has been the leading community to hear from customers and has grown into the best destination with the most selection of curated and new brands. All our efforts drive to one thing: bringing the best in the category to consumers. We're proud of the brands we're building that represent the next generation in the category.
